Official app information

Govt. Exam Pro

Practice support for selected Indian government recruitment examinations, with English-language questions, explanations and study progress kept on your device.

Privacy policySupport

About the app

Govt. Exam Pro is an exam-practice app. It is not affiliated with the Government of India, any recruiting body or any examination authority. It does not promise selection, ranks or examination outcomes.

The current internal-test build is an editorial preview. Questions, solutions and difficulty labels remain subject to independent review and calibration before a public release.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026. This policy applies to the Govt. Exam Pro Android app and this website.

Information handled on your device

The app stores your study progress, attempts, preferences and optional exported backups locally on your device. The delivered internal-test build has no sign-in, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or in-app payment collection. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

Optional online features

If an online question-reporting or AI-tutor feature is configured in a future release and you choose to use it, the selected question identifier, the text you enter and limited technical information needed to protect the service (such as a short-lived hashed IP-based quota signal) may be sent to the configured service. The current internal-test APK does not have those remote services configured.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Local records remain on your device until you delete the app data or remove them within the app. Exported files remain where you save them. Data sent through an optional future online feature would be retained only as needed to operate, secure and improve that feature.

Children

The app is designed for exam preparation and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can avoid optional online features, clear app data through Android settings and choose whether to create an export. Links opened in your browser are governed by the external site’s own terms and privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Support and privacy requests

For support, correction requests or privacy questions, open an issue in the official project repository: Govt. Exam Pro support.

Before a production Play release, the developer will add a dedicated support contact and complete Play Console’s Data safety, app-content and store-listing requirements.
